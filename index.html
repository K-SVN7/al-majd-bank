FAQ
What are the limits for international transfers?
Standard international wires are capped at QAR 50,000 per transaction and QAR 200,000 per day. Private banking clients can request higher limits directly from their relationship manager.
How long does an international transfer take?
SEPA (Europe): 1–2 business days. SWIFT (global): 2–5 business days. GCC transfers: same or next business day. Transfers submitted after 3 PM are processed the following business day.
How do I protect my account from fraud?
Enable Face ID and push notifications. Al Majd Bank will never ask for your PIN or full card number by phone or email. If you suspect fraud, freeze your card immediately and call +974 4411 0000.
Can I use my card abroad without fees?
Private banking clients enjoy zero foreign transaction fees on purchases. ATM withdrawals abroad incur a flat fee of QAR 15. Currency conversion uses the Visa interbank rate plus 0.5%.
How do I update my contact information?
Visit any Al Majd Bank branch with a valid Qatar ID, or contact your dedicated relationship manager. Address and phone changes require in-person verification for security.
